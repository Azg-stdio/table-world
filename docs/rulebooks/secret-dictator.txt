SECRET DICTATOR (the sandbox check for hidden-role games: plays like Secret Hitler, summarised in our words)

Players: 5 to 10.

Teams and roles
- Two teams: the Liberals and the Fascists. One of the fascists is the Dictator.
- Team sizes: 5 players 3 liberals / 2 fascists (the Dictator and 1 more); 6: 4/2; 7: 4/3; 8: 5/3; 9: 5/4; 10: 6/4.
- The fascists other than the Dictator know who all the fascists are, the Dictator included.
- With 5 or 6 players, the Dictator also knows the other fascist. With 7 or more, the Dictator knows nobody.

The policy deck
- 17 policy cards: 6 liberal and 11 fascist, shuffled face down.
- When fewer than 3 cards are left to draw, the discards are shuffled back in.

A round
- 1. The president (moving clockwise each round) nominates a chancellor.
  - The president cannot nominate themselves.
  - They cannot nominate the last elected chancellor.
  - With more than 5 players alive, they also cannot nominate the last elected president.
- 2. Everyone alive votes openly yes or no.
  - A majority of yes elects the government. A tie fails.
  - If 3 or more fascist policies are enacted and the Dictator is elected chancellor, the fascists win at once.
  - A failed vote moves the election tracker. At 3 failed elections in a row, the top policy is enacted as it is and
    the tracker resets.
- 3. Legislation.
  - The president draws 3 policies, discards 1 in secret, and passes the other 2 to the chancellor.
  - The chancellor enacts 1 and discards the other.
  - Nobody else sees the cards. Players may say anything about them, true or false.
- 4. When the 4th and the 5th fascist policies are enacted, the president executes a player.
  - The executed player is out. Their role is not shown, unless they were the Dictator: then the liberals win.

The end
- The liberals win with 5 liberal policies, or when the Dictator is executed.
- The fascists win with 6 fascist policies, or when the Dictator is elected chancellor after 3 fascist policies.

Left out of this version: investigations, special elections, the policy peek and the veto.
