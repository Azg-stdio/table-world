WINK MURDER (a party game, summarised for Table World's tests)

Players: 5 to 12.

Roles
- One Murderer. The host may add a Detective. Everyone else is a Guest.
- Nobody knows anyone else's role.

Playing
- There are no turns. Everyone sits around the table, talks and looks at each other's faces.
- The Murderer kills by winking at a player who is looking at them. Nobody else must notice.
- A player who has been winked at waits a moment, then falls dramatically and is out. Their role is shown.
- The Detective (or, with no Detective in the game or once they are dead, any player) may accuse someone at any time.
  - If the accused is the Murderer, the town wins.
  - If not, the accuser is out, and their role is shown.

The end
- The town wins when the Murderer is caught.
- The Murderer wins when only one other player is left.
