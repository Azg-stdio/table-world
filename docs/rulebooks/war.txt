WAR - a card game for two players

Equipment: one standard 52-card deck. Aces are high (A K Q J 10 ... 2).

Setup: shuffle the deck and deal it out face down, so each player has a pile of 26 cards.

Play: both players turn over the top card of their pile at the same time. The player whose card is higher
takes both cards and puts them face down at the bottom of their pile.

War: if the two cards are equal, there is a war. Each player puts one card face down, then turns over another
card. The higher of the new face-up cards takes all the cards on the table. If they are equal again, the war
continues the same way.

End: the player who collects all 52 cards wins.
