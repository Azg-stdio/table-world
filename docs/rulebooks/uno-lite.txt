MATCH THE PILE (a simplified UNO with a standard deck), 2 to 4 players

Equipment: one standard 52-card deck, no jokers.

Setup: shuffle the deck and deal 7 cards to each player. Put the rest face down as the draw pile and turn
its top card face up to start the discard pile.

Your turn: play one card from your hand onto the discard pile. It must match the top card of the discard
pile in suit or in rank. If you cannot or do not want to play, draw one card instead. Either way your turn
ends and play passes to the left.

The draw pile: when it runs out, shuffle the discard pile except its top card to make a new draw pile.

End: the first player with no cards in hand wins.
