WEREWOLF (a common folk version, summarised for Table World's tests)

Players: 6 to 12. A narrator runs it at a real table; in Table World the rules do, and the host may sit out as narrator.

Teams and roles
- Two teams: the Villagers and the Werewolves.
- Werewolves: 2 with 6 to 8 players, 3 with 9 to 12.
- One Seer among the villagers. The host may add a Doctor. Everyone else is a Villager.
- The werewolves know each other. Nobody else knows anything.

The night (everyone else's eyes closed)
- 1. The werewolves wake and agree on one player to kill. Only they know whom they chose.
- 2. The Seer wakes and looks at one player's card. The Seer learns only that player's side.
- 3. The Doctor, if in the game, wakes and protects one player for the night. They may protect themselves.
- A dead Seer or Doctor no longer wakes.

Dawn
- If the werewolves' victim was not protected, that player dies. Their card is shown to everyone.
- Otherwise nobody died.

The day
- Everyone alive talks, then votes openly for a player to lynch.
- The player with the most votes dies, and their card is shown. On a tie, nobody dies.
- The dead no longer speak or vote.

The end
- The villagers win when every werewolf is dead.
- The werewolves win when they are as many as the villagers left alive.
