AVALON (summary written for Table World's tests; plays like The Resistance: Avalon)

Players: 5 to 10.

Teams and roles
- Two teams: the Loyal servants of Arthur (good) and the Minions of Mordred (evil).
- Team sizes by player count: 5 players 3 good / 2 evil; 6: 4/2; 7: 4/3; 8: 5/3; 9: 6/3; 10: 6/4.
- Always in play: Merlin (good) and the Assassin (evil). The other good players are Loyal servants, the other evil
  players Minions of Mordred.
- Optional roles the host may add: Percival (good), Morgana, Mordred and Oberon (evil). They replace a Loyal servant or
  a Minion.
- At the start, each player secretly learns their role.
  - The evil players learn who the other evil players are, except Oberon.
  - Merlin learns who the evil players are, except Mordred.
  - Oberon learns nobody, and the other evil players do not learn him.
  - Percival learns which two players are Merlin and Morgana, but not which is which.

A round
- The leader moves clockwise every round; the first leader is chosen at random.
- 1. The leader picks a team for the current quest. The team's size depends on the quest and the player count:
       5 players: 2, 3, 2, 3, 3        6 players: 2, 3, 4, 3, 4        7 players: 2, 3, 3, 4, 4
       8, 9 or 10 players: 3, 4, 4, 5, 5
- 2. Everyone votes, openly, to approve or reject the team.
  - A majority of approvals sends the team on the quest. A tie rejects it.
  - If the team is rejected, the next player becomes leader and proposes again.
  - Five rejected teams in a row: evil wins.
- 3. The team members secretly choose success or fail. Good players must choose success; evil players may choose
     either. Only the number of fails is revealed, not who played them.
  - The quest fails with one fail card.
  - Exception: with 7 or more players, the fourth quest fails only with two fail cards.
- After the quest, the next player becomes leader.

The end
- Three failed quests: evil wins at once.
- Three successful quests: the Assassin names one good player.
  - If it is Merlin, evil wins.
  - Otherwise good wins.

Questions the rulebook leaves open (asked of the host when building)
- Can the leader include themselves in the team? Yes.
- Is the rejection count reset after a quest goes ahead? Yes, it resets once a team is approved.
- Does the Assassin choose alone or with the evil team's advice? Alone in the rules (talking is free at the table).
